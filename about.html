Our Story

About Bean Boutique

Since 2014, we've been roasting exceptional coffee and building a community that celebrates craft, connection, and the perfect cup.

Who We Are

More Than a Coffee Shop

Bean Boutique began as a small roastery on Roastery Lane with a simple belief: great coffee brings people together. What started as a neighbourhood café has grown into a destination for artisan roasts, curated brewing equipment, and hands-on workshops.

Every bean we roast is ethically sourced through direct-trade relationships with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Kenya. We roast in small batches weekly to ensure peak freshness and flavour.
